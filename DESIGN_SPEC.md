# Design Specification: Industry and Contract System

This document outlines the agreed-upon design for the new industry and contract system. This will serve as the blueprint for implementation.

## I. Core Philosophy

The simulation will be driven by a **contract-based economy**, not a micro-managed inventory system. The core gameplay loop is for the player to accept and fulfill long-term contracts that are generated based on high-level industry capacities.

## II. Industry (`Node`) Properties

Each industry will have the following key properties:

-   **`base_capacity` (float):** The total production/consumption capacity of the industry, measured in `cars/month`.
-   **`business_days` (list of strings):** A list of days the industry is open for business (e.g., `['mon', 'tue', 'wed', 'thu', 'fri']`).
-   **`service_rating` (float):** A rating from 0 to 100, starting at 50. This will increase with good service and decrease with poor service.
-   **`active_contracts` (list of Contract objects):** A list of all contracts currently active for this industry. The sum of the `cars_per_month` of these contracts cannot exceed the industry's `base_capacity`.

### Dynamic Capacity

An industry's effective capacity can grow over time. If the `service_rating` for an industry is consistently high, its `base_capacity` may permanently increase, allowing it to offer more/larger contracts.

## III. Contract (`Contract`) Properties

Contracts are the central object of the new system.

-   **`origin_id` / `destination_id` (int):** The start and end points.
-   **`cargo_type` (string):** The commodity to be moved.
-   **`cars_per_month` (int):** The number of carloads this contract generates per 30-day period.
-   **`total_duration_months` (int):** The total length of the contract.
-   **`revenue_per_car` (int):** The base payment for each successfully delivered carload.
-   **`status` (string):** The state of the contract (`offered`, `active`, `expired`, `cancelled`).
-   **`acceptance_deadline_tick` (int):** The game tick by which an `offered` contract must be accepted.
-   **`expiration_tick` (int):** The game tick when an `active` contract expires.
-   **`deadline_service_hours` (int):** The time limit for an empty car to be delivered to the origin.
-   **`deadline_delivery_hours` (int):** The time limit for a loaded car to be delivered to the destination.

## IV. Waybill (`Waybill`) Properties & Lifecycle

A Waybill represents a single, specific carload request generated by an active Contract.

### Properties

-   **`waybill_id` (int):** Unique identifier.
-   **`contract_id` (int):** The parent contract.
-   **`state` (string):** The current stage of the delivery.
-   **`creation_tick` (int):** The tick it was created.
-   **`service_due_tick` (int):** The calculated deadline for empty car delivery.
-   **`delivery_due_tick` (int):** The calculated deadline for loaded car delivery.

### Lifecycle (States)

1.  **`Pending`**: Generated by the contract. Awaiting player action.
2.  **`Servicing`**: Player has assigned a train; empty car is en-route to origin.
3.  **`Loading`**: Empty car has arrived and is being loaded.
4.  **`WaitingForPickup`**: Car is loaded and waiting for a train.
5.  **`InTransit`**: Loaded car is on a train to the destination.
6.  **`Unloading`**: Loaded car has arrived and is being unloaded.
7.  **`Completed`**: Delivery finished. Status (on-time/late) is recorded.

## V. Game Logic & Calculations

-   **Calendar:** The game will use an uninterrupted 7-day week (Mon-Sun) and 30-day months for all calculations.
-   **Daily Car Generation:** On an industry's business day, it will generate carloads based on its active contracts. The number of cars per day will be calculated from the contract's `cars_per_month` rate, adjusted by a **30% variance** using a normal distribution.
-   **Penalties:** Missing either the `service_due_tick` or `delivery_due_tick` will result in penalties. These penalties will be tied to the contract's commodity type (e.g., more severe for time-sensitive goods) and may include revenue loss or contract cancellation.
